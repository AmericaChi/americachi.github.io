L. América Chi

Senior Postdoctoral Researcher · Ytreberg Lab, Department of Physics, University of Idaho

Email Google Scholar ORCID GitHub CV (PDF)

I am a computational scientist with a background in physical engineering and a PhD in Physical Chemistry (CINVESTAV, Mexico). I build molecular models (molecular dynamics, enhanced sampling, docking, and machine learning) to study biomolecules, biomaterials, and drug candidates. I am currently a senior postdoctoral researcher in the Ytreberg Lab (PI: F. Marty Ytreberg) at the University of Idaho, where I work on the computational design of antimalarial compounds (PROTACs) in a U.S. Department of Defense–funded project. In parallel, I am laying the groundwork for my future independent group, the Computational Biophysics of Biomolecular Interactions (CBBI) Lab.

Research interests (more →)

  • Neural-network corrections of binding free-energy predictions for protein mutations
  • AI-based structural models of host–virus interfaces and viral escape
  • Peptide design, peptide–nanocarrier (PAMAM dendrimer) complexes, and peptide oligomerization
  • Membrane proteins: CLC transporters and cone opsins
  • Targeted protein degradation and structure-based drug design
